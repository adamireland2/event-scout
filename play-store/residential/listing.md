**Full description** (4000 characters max):

    Event Scout Residential is an AI-powered event finder built for tax
    incentive professionals who sell into residential real estate.

    Pick a category, choose your dates and cities, and get a ranked,
    researched list of in-person conferences and networking events where
    residential decision makers will actually be in the room — production
    homebuilders, multifamily developers, build-to-rent operators, SFR
    portfolio investors, and the CPAs who serve them.

    WHAT IT FINDS
    • Homebuilder association events (NAHB, BIA, HBA)
    • Multifamily and build-to-rent conferences
    • Real estate investor association (REIA) and SFR events
    • Tax & CPA society conferences
    • Member organization calendars you specify

    EVERY RESULT INCLUDES
    • Date, venue, organizer, and cost
    • A priority ranking (Must-Attend / Strong Fit / Worth It)
    • Why the event matters for your pipeline (45L, cost seg, 179D fit)
    • Which decision makers will be there
    • Suggested action items and one-tap add to Google Calendar

    BUILT FOR THE FIELD
    • In-person events only — no webinars, ever
    • Budget mode keeps AI search costs to pennies
    • Recent searches are saved on your device and free to reopen
    • Print or export a clean one-page event brief

    Bring your own Anthropic API key (created free at console.anthropic.com;
    searches cost a few cents each in API credits). Your key and your search
    history stay on your device — nothing is collected by us.

    Selling into commercial real estate too? Get our companion app,
    Event Scout CRE.
